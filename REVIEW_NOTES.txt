   and finite-time effects, and that boundedness does not rule out all temporal

   clarified how to share a combined leading speed-error budget.

   included the threshold equality case. Low K with positive r is not intrinsically
   lethal habitat; immigration above local K is source-sink-like redistribution.

    finite no-flux domains eventually saturate.
11. Declared minimum dependency versions (np.trapezoid requires NumPy >=2.0),
